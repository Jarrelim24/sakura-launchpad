How Sakura Works

The same launch mechanics that went viral — built fair from the very first second.

1

Create in 1 transaction

Enter a name, ticker, description, and logo. The full fixed supply of 1 billion tokens goes straight onto the bonding curve and trading starts immediately. Launch fee: just 0.0005 LOOP.

2

Trade on the curve

Buy and sell against the bonding curve — price rises as people buy, falls as they sell. Anti-snipe: a 99% buy tax in the first second decays to 0% within ±5 seconds, so bots can't front-run real people.

3

Graduate to Uniswap

Once the curve collects 4.2 LOOP, the token graduates automatically: liquidity moves to Uniswap and is locked permanently / burned — no one can pull it, not even the creator.

The Bonding Curve, in Plain Words

Picture a glass filling with water. Early on, the price is low because the curve is nearly empty. Every buy fills it a little more and nudges the price up — smoothly, not in random jumps. The dashed line at the top is graduation: reach it, and the token moves to the open Uniswap market.

After graduation, every pool swap pays 0.3% — 0.20% stays in the pool, 0.05% goes to the token’s creator and 0.05% to the platform — plus the token’s own creator tax (1–3%, locked at launch) entirely to the creator. The creator and platform shares collect in the same FeeVault and Treasury balances and are claimed together in Profile. Pairs created before the fee-split upgrade keep the original model: the full 0.3% stays in the pool.
